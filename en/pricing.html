Frequently asked questions

Before you ask.

My video is longer than 45 minutes. What should I do?

Say so in your request and tell us which part interests you. We will suggest a suitable solution.

Which languages do you work in?

Our editing workflow is currently tested on French-language videos. For a video in another language, English included, tell us in your request: we will confirm whether we can take it on before anything starts.

Who chooses the passages?

We suggest a selection based on the transcript. You can also give exact timestamps in your notes.

Which file formats do you accept?

The usual formats: MP4, MOV, MKV or WEBM, ideally 1080p or higher.

What happens to my files?

They are used only to fulfil your order and are deleted after the delivery period. They are never shared or used to train a model.

Can I ask for changes?

The pilot session includes one minor revision, for example to the subtitle text or a cut point.
