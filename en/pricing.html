Frequently asked questions

Before you ask.

My video is longer than 45 minutes. What should I do?

Say so in your request and tell us which part interests you (45 minutes at most). For several videos or a longer source, request a quote.

Which languages do you work in?

Our editing workflow is currently tested on French-language videos. For a video in another language, English included, tell us in your request: we will confirm whether we can take it on before anything starts.

Who chooses the passages?

We suggest a selection based on the transcript. You can also give exact timestamps in your notes.

Which files can I send?

The usual formats: MP4, MOV, MKV or WEBM, ideally 1080p or higher. Don't email us any file: after your request, we tell you how to send it privately.

Who holds the rights?

You must hold the rights to the video and the consent of the people who appear in it. The delivered clips are yours; we never show them anywhere without your written consent.

How are the subtitles made?

They are burned into the picture, proofread and synchronised: dynamic (word by word), understated (sentence by sentence) or none. Translation is not included.

Can I ask for changes?

The pilot session includes one minor revision, for example to the subtitle text or a cut point.

How is delivery handled?

Three vertical MP4 files (1080 × 1920) through a private, temporary link. We confirm the turnaround by email; during the pilot phase, no turnaround is guaranteed.

What happens to my files?

They are used only to fulfil your order and are deleted after the delivery period. They are never shared or used to train a model.
