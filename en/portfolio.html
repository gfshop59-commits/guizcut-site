Technical sheet

What every clip contains.

    Vertical MP4

  • Length

    30 to 60 seconds

    One idea per clip, with a clear opening and a clean ending.

  • Text

    Burned-in subtitles

    Readable without sound, proofread before delivery.

    Normalised volume

    Consistent level from one clip to the next, checked automatically.
