How it works

From long video to clips.

Six steps, assisted by our analysis tools and checked by a human before every delivery.

The steps

  1. You

    You make your request

    Describe your video, its length, the target platform and your notes through the form.

  2. You

    You send the video

    A file or a download link. Format, length and sound are checked before we start.

  3. The studio

    We find the passages

    The video is transcribed, then the excerpts that stand on their own are identified and reviewed.

  4. The studio

    We edit the 3 clips

    Vertical 9:16 reframing, tight editing, proofread synchronised subtitles, cleaned-up normalised sound.

  5. The studio

    We check

    Length, resolution, codec, sound level and subtitles are checked for each clip.

  6. You

    You download your MP4s

    Through a private, temporary link. One minor revision is included.

Our editing workflow is currently tested on French-language videos. For a video in another language, English included, tell us in your request: we will confirm whether we can take it on before anything starts.

Who does what

Tools prepare, humans decide.

Our tools

  • File check: format, length, audio tracks
  • Timestamped text to find every passage
  • Shortlist of candidate passages
  • 9:16 reframing, subtitles and normalised sound
  • Automatic checks: resolution, length, codec, volume

Our editor

  • Final choice of the three passages
  • Word-by-word subtitle proofreading
  • Fine-tuning of cuts and pace
  • Sign-off before every delivery
  • Exchanges with you, by email

No promise of views or virality: we deliver a careful edit that you publish wherever you like.

Commitments

Your content stays yours.

Private files

Accessible only to the editing team, through time-limited links.

Scheduled deletion

Sources and clips are deleted at the end of the delivery period.

You remain the owner

You keep all rights to your videos and to the delivered clips. Nothing is used to train a model.
