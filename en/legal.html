Privacy

The site sets no GUIZCUT cookies and uses Plausible Analytics for aggregated audience measurement, without advertising profiling. Depending on the mode shown on the Contact page, the form either prepares an email in your own email app, or sends your request to contact@guizcut.fr through the form-forwarding service Web3Forms, which relays it by email without publishing it. On the home page and the Showcase page, the thumbnails of the reference Shorts are displayed from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com), which therefore receive the visitor's IP address. The videos themselves play in YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com), loaded only when you start a video. The information you send us by email is used only to answer your request.
