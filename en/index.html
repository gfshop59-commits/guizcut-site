The shoot

Your video, in five shots.

From upload to delivery, every step is framed. Our tools assist the analysis, and every clip is checked before it leaves the editing room.

  1. Intake

    You send the long video. Format, length and sound are checked.

  2. Transcript

    Every sentence is timestamped so the video reads like a script.

  3. Selection

    Three passages that stand on their own: hook, idea, payoff.

  4. Edit

    9:16 reframing, burned-in subtitles, tight cuts, normalised sound.

  5. Screening

    Quality check, then your MP4s through a private, temporary link.

Frequently asked questions

Before you send.

Which files can I send?

One video of up to 45 minutes, in the usual formats: MP4, MOV, MKV or WEBM, ideally 1080p or higher. Don't email us any file: after your request, we tell you how to send it privately.

Who holds the rights?

You must hold the rights to the video and the consent of the people who appear in it. The delivered clips are yours; we never show them anywhere without your written consent.

How are the subtitles made?

They are burned into the picture, proofread and synchronised: dynamic (word by word), understated (sentence by sentence) or none. Our workflow is currently tested on French-language videos; for another language, tell us in your request and we will confirm before anything starts. Translation is not included.

Can I ask for changes?

One minor revision is included, for example to a subtitle's text or a cut point. Just reply to the delivery email.

How is delivery handled?

Three vertical MP4 files (1080 × 1920), sent through a private, temporary link. We confirm the turnaround by email, depending on your video; during the pilot phase, no turnaround is guaranteed.

What happens to my source files?

They are used only for your order, never shared or used to train a model, and deleted at the end of the delivery period.

Breakdown

What makes a Short hold.

The ingredients we work on in every clip, observed on an external reference: LEGEND's Short with Philippe Boxho.

  1. 01

    A hook from the very first second

    The question appears on screen before the answer: viewers know why they should stay.

  2. 02

    Tight 9:16 framing

    The face fills the vertical screen, with nothing superfluous around it.

  3. 03

    Word-by-word subtitles

    Readable without sound, key words in colour, placed above the apps' interface.

  4. 04

    One idea, one payoff

    A single topic of 30 to 60 seconds that stands on its own and ends cleanly.

  5. 05

    Clean sound

    Voice up front, consistent level from one clip to the next.
