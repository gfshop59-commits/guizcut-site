Who we are

GUIZCUT, a vertical editing studio.

We turn creators' long videos (streams, podcasts, YouTube videos) into subtitled Shorts, Reels and TikToks, ready to post.

Our method in video

From one long video to three clips.

GUIZCUT is a vertical editing studio for creators who want to publish consistently without spending their evenings cutting their videos.

We find the moments worth watching, reframe them to 9:16 and turn them into clear, paced clips ready for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

  • Human selection of passages with a real hook.
  • Readable editing with proofread subtitles, cleaned sound and checked framing.
  • Simple delivery of three ready-to-post MP4s, with one revision included.

What we do

One craft: the vertical clip.

Shorts, Reels and TikTok editing for streamers, YouTubers and podcasters, from one long video of up to 45 minutes.

Select

The video is transcribed and timestamped; we keep the passages that stand on their own: a hook, an idea, a payoff.

Edit

9:16 reframing, tight cuts, burned-in subtitles proofread word by word, cleaned-up and normalised sound.

Check

Length, resolution, codec, sound level and subtitles are checked for every clip before delivery.

Our principles

Tools prepare, humans decide.

Our tools check the files, timestamp the text and prepare candidate passages. The final choice, the subtitle proofreading and the sign-off of every clip are down to our editor.

Your rights

You keep all rights to your videos and to the delivered clips. We never show a clip anywhere without your written consent.

Your files

Only accessible to the editing team, never used to train a model, deleted at the end of the delivery period.

No promises

We deliver careful editing, with no guarantee of views, subscribers or virality.
